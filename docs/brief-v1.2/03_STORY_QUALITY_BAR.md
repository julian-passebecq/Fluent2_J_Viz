# Story quality bar

A flagship story is not just several charts in sequence.

Each story should have:
- one clear analytical question;
- message-first title;
- concise subtitle/takeaway;
- deliberate scene progression;
- direct labels where possible;
- sparse legend;
- annotations tied to evidence;
- source/note;
- readable paused states;
- meaningful motion only;
- phone-specific annotation simplification.

A story should still communicate if autoplay is never pressed.
